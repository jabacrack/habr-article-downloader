Privacy Policy — Habr Article Downloader

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Summary

Habr Article Downloader does not collect analytics or sell user data. Article conversion happens locally in your browser. At your request, articles can also be sent to your local Joplin application.

What the extension does

The extension converts publications from habr.com into Markdown (.md) files and saves them to your device via the browser's standard download mechanism. It can do this on demand, in batches, or on a schedule based on filters you configure.

Data storage

  • Settings (filters, filename template, watch sources, UI options) are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local.
  • Download journal — a local list of IDs of already-downloaded publications, used only to avoid duplicate downloads. Stored locally.
  • Joplin connection — the local Web Clipper token, pending authorization and last selected notebook are stored locally. The token is excluded from settings exports.

These settings and the journal are not uploaded to the developer. There are no analytics or trackers and no developer-operated backend. The extension does not require its own account; a Habr login is needed for personalized feeds.

Network requests

The extension fetches publications and feeds from habr.com. If you enable Joplin integration and grant optional local host access, it contacts the Web Clipper service at 127.0.0.1 on ports 41184–41194 to authorize, list notebooks and save the selected article, title and source URL. Joplin handles any further synchronization according to your Joplin settings.

Requests to Habr include the requested URLs and may include your existing Habr session cookies so that personalized feeds work. Habr and, when downloading images locally, image hosting servers receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address. The Joplin token is sent only to the local Web Clipper service. Firefox declares website content, browsing activity and authentication information to describe these functional transfers; this does not mean that the developer receives your browsing history or credentials.

Permissions

  • downloads — saving .md files to your device;
  • storage — local settings and the duplicate-prevention journal;
  • alarms — the optional scheduled watch feature;
  • contextMenus — optional right-click menu items on habr.com;
  • notifications — optional completion notifications;
  • optional host access to http://127.0.0.1/* — connecting to your local Joplin Web Clipper service;
  • host access to https://habr.com/* — the extension's single purpose is working with Habr.

Third parties

No data is sold. Functional requests go to Habr, image hosts when local image downloads are enabled, and optionally your local Joplin application as described above. No data is used by the extension for advertising or any purpose unrelated to saving Habr publications.
